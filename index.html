FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form on this page, call 061 524 9438 or 064 901 7759, or WhatsApp us your pick-up address, drop-off address and a list of items. We usually reply the same day.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Stilfontein and serve Klerksdorp, Orkney, Hartbeesfontein, Potchefstroom, Fochville, Carletonville and the wider North West. Long-distance moves to Johannesburg, Pretoria and beyond are available on request.

Do you move single items or only full houses?

Both. We move full households and offices, and we also collect and deliver single items like a fridge, couch, bed or washing machine.

Can I book on short notice?

Often, yes. Contact us as early as possible with your preferred date and we'll confirm availability. Weekends and month-end fill up fast, so book early where you can.

How is the price calculated?

Pricing depends on the volume of items, the distance between addresses, access (stairs, parking, lifts) and the number of movers needed. You'll get a clear quote before the move.

Do you help with loading, offloading and packing?

Yes. Our team loads, secures and offloads your belongings and places furniture in the rooms you choose. Ask about packing assistance when you request a quote.
